Who we are

What we stand for

Love & Logic for Canada

Love without logic is sentiment. Logic without love of country is a cold machine. We hold both — for citizens first.

The headliner — ultimate governance

Universal Decision Logic is how every material decision of government will be made and tested. Not a slogan. The operating system of the state under the Maple Leaf.

Every law, tax, border rule, program, and major spend must answer: does this make Canadian citizens — and their children — safer, smarter, stronger, and richer? The record must steelman the case for and against, from every end of the political spectrum, with history in the room. Data must be replicable and free of foreign capture. World-leading, truth-seeking AI deep-analyzes key decisions on national and provincial supercomputers; a Human Council monitors the machines; named humans still decide.

That is the supreme method. All else answers to it. Read it in full on the plan and in the platform framework.

How we govern

  • Universal Decision Logic: bulletproof method on every material act — the headliner.
  • Citizens first: the only pass mark; borders, trade, and speech serve that centre.
  • Steelman & history: all sides, all priors; no one-spectrum theatre.
  • Truth-seeking AI under humans: extreme compute; human council; no social-credit state.
  • Fully auditable: public treasury, open contracts, money and decisions you can re-check.
  • Merit: one law; hire and spend by competence. No preference by race or by the politics of sex.

What follows

From this method come open books, AI audit of waste, secure borders, honest food, rules on vice by harm, family tax power, national service instead of drift, and a long path to surplus and a hard dollar. The detail is in the Complete Plan. The root is the method.
