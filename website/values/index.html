Who we are

What we stand for

Love & Logic for Canada

Love without logic is sentiment. Logic without love of country is a cold machine. We hold both — for citizens first.

The supreme priority

Every decision of government must rest on logic and critical thought, and aim at a safer, smarter, stronger, and richer quality of life for Canadian citizens first and foremost. That is the supreme priority. All else answers to it.

How we govern

  • Logic: laws must survive cause and effect, incentives, and arithmetic — not the weather of fashion.
  • Critical thought: we name trade-offs; we do not sell free lunch forever.
  • Citizens first: the centre of the law is the citizen. Borders, trade, and speech serve that centre.
  • Fully auditable: a public treasury, open contracts, money that can be followed. Government you can check yourself.
  • Merit: one law; hire and spend by competence. No preference by race or by the politics of sex.

What follows

From this standard come open books, secure borders, honest food, rules on vice by harm, family tax power, national service instead of drift, and a long path to surplus and a hard dollar. The detail is in the Complete Plan. The root is here.
