What we stand on

How we decide

The emphasis is logic. Extreme logical scrutiny on every material decision. No fashion filter.

Canada already has pieces of this: public records, impact notes, consultations. We extend that idea so it covers everything that matters (laws, taxes, borders, programs, contracts, major spends), not a handful of showpiece files. Every one of those decisions gets a public Decision Package.

Each package is tested hard: pure logic, history of what was tried and what failed, the strongest case for and against from every side of the political spectrum, and evidence that can be checked. A named person still signs. Feelings alone do not write law.

The analysis is uncensored. We cannot afford censored viewpoints when Canadian livelihoods are at stake. Hide a risk, ban a question, or protect a sacred idea from challenge, and citizens pay for the lie. Tools may help stress-test claims; they do not hold the pen. When better evidence arrives, the package is revised in the open.

What we propose

Open government

This is the hard proposal. Open the state. Open the books. Let citizens see, question, and help design.

The whole civilian state is open source and auditable. The only hard secrets are military operations and secret intelligence work under cleared oversight. Nothing else gets to hide.

Today this living model sits on GitHub so anyone can read and propose while the party is built. Under a Maple government Canada runs a national open-government platform of its own, not a foreign commercial host. Proposals, waste reports, and chamber access require a verified Canadian citizen identity account in the spirit of My Service Canada, citizens only, not bots and not the world.

Donors and contracts are public. Walk it on the interactive Treasury map.

Any public dollar requested to leave the country faces the same sunlight, only stricter. No quiet foreign send without public analysis and a Decision Package that shows why Canadians benefit.

Citizen waste bounty pays you when a verified report actually cuts tax dollars. For the people, by the people, in the files and in the chamber.

Under debate

We are not proposing a finished statute book. Tax design, health, borders, food rules, service year, and the rest are topics and draft arguments for public Decision Packages — open to challenge, revision, or rejection under the method.

  • Family tax and the cost of children
  • Food truth and advertising to kids
  • Borders, capacity, and security
  • Healthcare incentives and capacity
  • Service year, harm online, and more

Read them as questions, not as law. Only the decision method and open government are firm.
