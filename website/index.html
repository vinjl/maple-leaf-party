Philosophy first

Why we exist

The supreme purpose of government is life quality excellence for Canadians. Not donor fashion, not identity theatre, not applause abroad at the expense of people who live here and pay the bills. Every law, tax, border rule, and budget line is judged by whether it makes citizens safer, smarter, stronger, and richer, including the next generation.

That standard is applied with extreme logic and critical thought. We steelman the case for and against from every side of the spectrum with history in the room, we grade data so only the replicable holds, we treat foreign-controlled sources as suspect, and we drop what fails the citizen test. Feelings alone do not write law. Named humans still sign.

Logical love for Canada means both things at once. Love without logic is sentiment. Logic without love of country is a cold machine. We hold both, for the people, by the people.

How the state will work

Open government

Simple rule. You can see the machine, follow every public dollar, and take part for real.

The whole civilian state is open source and auditable. The only hard secrets are military operations and secret intelligence work under cleared oversight. Nothing else gets to hide.

Today this living model sits on GitHub so anyone can read and propose while the party is built. Under a Maple government Canada runs a national open-government platform of its own, not a foreign commercial host. Proposals, waste reports, and chamber access require a verified Canadian citizen identity account in the spirit of My Service Canada, citizens only, not bots and not the world.

Donors and contracts are public. Walk it on the interactive Treasury map.

Any public dollar requested to leave the country faces the same sunlight, only stricter. No quiet foreign send without public analysis and a Decision Package that shows why Canadians benefit.

Citizen waste bounty pays you when a verified report actually cuts tax dollars. For the people, by the people, in the files and in the chamber.

What follows from the philosophy

Key decisions

Once citizens first and open logic are fixed, these are the conclusions on Canadian life. Short here, fuller on the plan. Improve any of it through the open-government path.

Family tax — headline

Three dependent children, parents married or common-law together: zero personal income tax for both parents. Deep cuts on the path at one and two kids. Nation-building households keep more of what they earn. See the Decision Package → · Plan detail

Borders

Intake tracks real capacity (housing, hospitals, wages). Prefer high skill and security clearance. Asylum is not a mass loophole. Violent non-citizens are removed. Residual risk to citizens fails the test. Decision Package → · Plan

Healthcare

Under 25 it is free no matter what, while the brain is still developing. After that it is your responsibility, because that is when people are truly accountable for preventable disease risk. It is unfair for the neighbour who trains daily, eats clean, and barely uses the system to keep paying the bill for people who will not try. Adults who keep basic health effort stay on the free public catastrophic pool, and emergency care always stabilizes first. If you refuse that effort, non-emergency care is on you through private care, insurance, or cash, funded by the tax room a leaner public system creates. Private industry must compete and can fail, so it is driven to better, faster, cheaper care, unlike a public monopoly that gets bailed out by taxpayers. We just pay the public bill when you have shown good health habits. Full explanation

Food

Full disclosure of what is in food. Ban ads for ultra-processed and clearly unhealthy products — adults may still buy; we ban the industrial pitch. Whole food tax-favoured. Decision Package → · Plan

Vices — ages, legalization, porn

Ages by harm, not fashion. 25+: alcohol; smoked/vaped nicotine; cannabis; LSD, MDMA, ketamine (purity, unit dose, tracked supply). 21+: psilocybin (regulated); smokeless tobacco. Psilocybin is a major correction: far lower dependence and overdose risk than alcohol, with real evidence of benefit in defined medical contexts — face that, end the hypocrisy. Commercial and public pornography banned; private non-commercial adult only. Gambling ads banned. Substances package → · Porn package → · Plan

Grade 10 becomes National Service Year

One mandatory year of military-style training replaces grade 10 — not combat deployment. Streams mostly sex-separated so people train instead of drama. Early rise, fitness, standards, real consequences. Why before grade 11–12: that is when habits lock; clean them up before university and trades are decided. Soft school discipline fails many boys at peak hormones stuck in chairs — channel that energy into becoming strong and reliable. Grade 11–12 still happen after, on a better baseline. An entire cohort that keeps time and finishes work changes the country. Decision Package → · Plan

Children’s clothing

Push kids’ garments toward organic and natural-fibre standards (and safer finishes) to cut needless chemical load on developing bodies. Phase in with clear lists. Plan

Waste bounty — the country fixes itself

Any verified citizen can report waste or fraud. If it checks out and tax dollars actually fall (clawback, cancel, stop-pay), they are paid — reward scaled to impact. Better than waiting four years for someone who does nothing major. Explorer issues & bounties → · Plan
