Logical love for Canada

The Maple Leaf Party of Canada

A federal political party · living draft

We want an auditable, logical government whose job is life quality excellence for Canadians (safer, smarter, stronger, richer for citizens and their children), so we apply hard logic to every decision with the reasoning published and the books open. Below is what that approach concludes, and you can walk a working model of open government too.

How power thinks

Logic, then law

Because Canadian life quality comes first, we put every decision through the same work, steelmanning the case for and against from every side of the spectrum with history in the room, grading the data so only the replicable holds and foreign-controlled sources stay suspect, and dropping what does not serve citizens.

World-class AI on national and provincial supercomputers stress-tests the hard files without political fashion filters, with prompts and outputs logged, a human council on the machines, and named officials still signing.

Open government means you can read the Decision Package yourself (official reasons, logic record, AI prompt and analysis, outcome, and where the money went) on the interactive state map and Treasury flow, with the full method on the plan.

What the method concludes

Key decisions

Same standard applied to Canadian life, short here and fuller on the plan and in the Explorer.

Family tax — headline

Three dependent children, parents married or common-law together: zero personal income tax for both parents. Deep cuts on the path at one and two kids. Nation-building households keep more of what they earn. See the Decision Package → · Plan detail

Borders

Intake tracks real capacity (housing, hospitals, wages). Prefer high skill and security clearance. Asylum is not a mass loophole. Violent non-citizens are removed. Residual risk to citizens fails the test. Decision Package → · Plan

Healthcare

Under 25 it is free no matter what, while the brain is still developing. After that it is your responsibility, because that is when people are truly accountable for preventable disease risk. It is unfair for the neighbour who trains daily, eats clean, and barely uses the system to keep paying the bill for people who will not try. Adults who keep basic health effort stay on the free public catastrophic pool (ER always stabilizes first). If you refuse that effort, non-emergency care is on you through private care, insurance, or cash, funded by the tax room a leaner public system creates. Private industry must compete and can fail, so it is driven to better, faster, cheaper care, unlike a public monopoly that gets bailed out by taxpayers. We just pay the public bill when you have shown good health habits. Full explanation

Food

Full disclosure of what is in food. Ban ads for ultra-processed and clearly unhealthy products — adults may still buy; we ban the industrial pitch. Whole food tax-favoured. Decision Package → · Plan

Vices — ages, legalization, porn

Ages by harm, not fashion. 25+: alcohol; smoked/vaped nicotine; cannabis; LSD, MDMA, ketamine (purity, unit dose, tracked supply). 21+: psilocybin (regulated); smokeless tobacco. Psilocybin is a major correction: far lower dependence and overdose risk than alcohol, with real evidence of benefit in defined medical contexts — face that, end the hypocrisy. Commercial and public pornography banned; private non-commercial adult only. Gambling ads banned. Substances package → · Porn package → · Plan

Grade 10 becomes National Service Year

One mandatory year of military-style training replaces grade 10 — not combat deployment. Streams mostly sex-separated so people train instead of drama. Early rise, fitness, standards, real consequences. Why before grade 11–12: that is when habits lock; clean them up before university and trades are decided. Soft school discipline fails many boys at peak hormones stuck in chairs — channel that energy into becoming strong and reliable. Grade 11–12 still happen after, on a better baseline. An entire cohort that keeps time and finishes work changes the country. Decision Package → · Plan

Children’s clothing

Push kids’ garments toward organic and natural-fibre standards (and safer finishes) to cut needless chemical load on developing bodies. Phase in with clear lists. Plan

Waste bounty — the country fixes itself

Any verified citizen can report waste or fraud. If it checks out and tax dollars actually fall (clawback, cancel, stop-pay), they are paid — reward scaled to impact. Better than waiting four years for someone who does nothing major. Explorer issues & bounties → · Plan
