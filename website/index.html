Who we are

What we stand for

In a few minutes you should know who we are — and feel proud to stand under the leaf.

The Maple Leaf is built on a single rule of government: government must think. Not by fashion, fear, or clique — but by logic and critical examination of cause and effect. The test of every law, tax, border rule, and budget line is simple: does this make life for Canadian citizens safer, smarter, stronger, and richer? If it does not, it fails. Citizens first is not a slogan; it is the whole measure.

We reject identity theatre, donor fog, and unreadable books. We demand a state that can be audited, that hires by merit, that admits who strengthens the country and removes who endangers it, that tells the truth about food and money, and that trains the young in discipline rather than leaving them to drift.

  • Citizens first — the supreme priority in every office of government.
  • Logic & critical thought — every decision must survive reason and evidence.
  • Open & auditable — sunlight on the treasury; no quiet rewriting of the ledger.
  • Merit — one law; competence over identity preference.

What follows

From principle to the life of the nation

Once you accept logic and citizens first, the rest unfolds with necessity. It is what naturally follows.

  • Open government — if citizens come first, they must see the books. Public treasury, open contracts, Trace on every dollar.
  • Secure borders & skilled entry — a people that will not guard the canopy loses the shade. Capacity, skill gaps only, violent non-citizens removed, law over any doctrine that licenses violence or inequality.
  • Health & food — truth on labels; ban ads for ultra-processed junk; do not socialize unlimited bad habits onto those who keep the covenant.
  • Vice — ban commercial porn and gambling ads; ages by harm. Less aggression, more productivity — especially for the young. Patience heals.
  • Tax & family — three dependent children, married or common-law together: zero income tax for both parents. Arithmetic that favours the household that builds the nation.
  • Service instead of grade 10 — one year of mandatory national service: military discipline, cohesion, order — a generation trained to finish what it starts.
  • Fiscal strength — cut waste, cut preventable load, cut crime cost; aim for immense surplus invested in a sovereign wealth fund; GDP per capita and a hard dollar, not empty headline GDP.

None of this is separate from the standard. It is the standard applied — to the pantry, the border, the school year, the ledger, and the long peace of a free people.
