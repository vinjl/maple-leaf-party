01 · The method

Universal Decision Logic

Government exists to raise life quality for Canadian citizens and their children above all else. That standard is applied with published logic — not slogans, not donor fashion.

Every material law, tax, border rule, contract, and budget line gets a public Decision Package: the official reason, the steelman for and against, history in the room, graded data (only the replicable holds), citizen-first scoring, and a named human who signs. Feelings alone do not write law. AI analysis is published under a human council; machines do not hold the pen.

When better evidence arrives, the package is revised in the open. Nothing is sacred because it was written once. Only what still survives the citizen test stays.

02 · Open books

Open government

Simple rule. You can see the machine, follow every public dollar, and take part for real. Federal power first: tax code, ledger, Criminal Code, border, federal contracts.

The whole civilian state is open source and auditable. The only hard secrets are military operations and secret intelligence work under cleared oversight. Nothing else gets to hide.

Today this living model sits on GitHub so anyone can read and propose while the party is built. Under a Maple government Canada runs a national open-government platform of its own, not a foreign commercial host. Proposals, waste reports, and chamber access require a verified Canadian citizen identity account in the spirit of My Service Canada, citizens only, not bots and not the world.

Donors and contracts are public. Walk it on the interactive Treasury map.

Any public dollar requested to leave the country faces the same sunlight, only stricter. No quiet foreign send without public analysis and a Decision Package that shows why Canadians benefit.

Citizen waste bounty pays you when a verified report actually cuts tax dollars. For the people, by the people, in the files and in the chamber.

03 · What we fight for first

Three federal commitments

Ottawa can deliver these without pretending to run the provinces. The full plan is published because we do not hide the rest.

1 · Open ledger, Trace IDs, open contracts

One Public Treasury. Every material outlay carries a Trace ID from vote to payment to outcome. Contracts and donor trails public. Falsification triggers investigation. Citizen waste bounty pays when a verified report actually cuts tax dollars. Treasury explorer → · Plan · Bounty

2 · Family tax

Deep personal-income-tax relief that scales with dependent children — designed so nation-building households keep more of what they earn. Target design: path to zero PIT at three dependents for co-parenting households, subject to published costing against the PIT base before statute. Charter-careful drafting; no uncosted slogan. Decision Package → · Plan detail

3 · Food truth and junk-food ads

Full disclosure of what is in food. Child-directed ad bans first (settled commercial-speech ground), then broader restrictions on ultra-processed marketing. Adults may still buy; we end the industrial pitch to kids. Decision Package → · Plan

More · honest framing

Federal power, provincial reality

Top-line promises are things a federal government controls: tax code, ledger, Criminal Code, border, federal contracts and transfers. Hospitals, grade curriculum, and retail licensing are provincial. Where we care about those outcomes, we say so — and frame federal tools as transfer conditions and incentives, slower and weaker than provincial statute.

Borders (federal)

Intake tracks real capacity (housing, hospitals, wages). Prefer high skill and security clearance. Asylum is not a mass loophole. Violent non-citizens are removed. Screening is conduct-based and applied to everyone — advocacy of violence, coercion, or defiance of the Criminal Code — not a faith-named loyalty test. Residual risk to citizens fails the test. Decision Package → · Plan

Healthcare (mostly provincial delivery)

Federal tools: Canada Health Act conditions, transfers, tax treatment, and published outcome data — not Ottawa running every ward. Goal: cut preventable load and expand capacity without a head-on repeal of universality. Under 25: full public cover while brains are still forming. For adults: fund prevention, publish results, tax-advantaged health savings where designed, and private capacity for elective care that competes on speed and price. Emergency care always stabilizes first. Exclusion gates that break the CHA are not the lead design. Full explanation

Harm online and on the shelf

Minors: strong age-verification and non-consensual content enforcement (achievable now). Gambling ads banned. Substance schedules and drinking ages are partly provincial; federal Criminal Code and import rules are the Ottawa lever. Harm ranking and purity rules will be phased and costed separately — not one soundbite that pairs “raise alcohol age” with “legalize MDMA.” Plan

National service (federal incentive, not a curriculum rewrite)

Grade 10 is provincial curriculum. Ottawa does not replace it by slogan. Lead design: a federally funded voluntary National Service Year with real incentives (tuition, trade credit, hiring preference). If the program is excellent, uptake proves it. Compulsion and curriculum substitution stay on the full plan only with provincial partners and Charter-careful drafting. Decision Package → · Plan

Waste bounty

Any verified citizen can report waste or fraud. If it checks out and tax dollars actually fall, they are paid — reward scaled to impact. Explorer → · Plan
