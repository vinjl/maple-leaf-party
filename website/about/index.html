About this party

Who we are · a living draft

The Maple Leaf Party of Canada is raised for logic-based, open-source, fully auditable government — for citizens, and for the Canadian dollar, first.

What we are

  • A party that leads with the plan — the whole design of the country, set in light.
  • Rooted in merit, equal citizenship, and a state you may audit with your own eyes.
  • Spoken in both of Canada’s public languages, English and French.

How we work

  • Policy written to be read, tested, and costed — not waved like a flag and forgotten.
  • Federal and provincial power named without fog. Provinces still keep the hospitals and the schools.
  • We invent no cabinets of shadows, no lists of names that are not yet real.

Registration

Not yet registered with Elections Canada. Until the formal path is walked, this is vision and gathering. We will not wear a title we have not earned.

Leadership

Leadership will be named when people of flesh and conscience are ready — not before, and not for show.
