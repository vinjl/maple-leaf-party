Ultimate governance — how every decision is made

Universal Decision Logic is the headliner of the Maple Leaf. It is not a white paper for the shelf. It is how power thinks before it acts — the same extreme standard for every material decision: laws, taxes, borders, programs, hires, grants, and major spends.

Fashion fails. Feelings theatre fails. One side of the spectrum talking only to itself fails. A single irreplicable study sold as permanent law fails. Under the Maple Leaf, a decision that cannot survive pure logic does not pass.

The test, every time

Does this make Canadian citizens — alive today, and their children — safer, smarter, stronger, and richer? If the honest answer is no, the proposal dies or is redesigned. Citizens first is the whole measure.

Steelmans — all sides, all history

For every decision type, the record must hold the strongest case for and the strongest case against — drawn without favour from every end of the political spectrum, and from what history actually shows: what was tried, what failed, what was tried again, where the money went, who paid the cost. Hidden assumptions named. Causal chains written so a competent outsider can check them. If the logic collapses when the slogans are stripped away, the decision fails.

Data that can be trusted

Is the claim replicable? Is the data under foreign control? Who funded the result? One secret paper is not enough for permanent law. Prior spend that moved no metric is not a reason to spend again “because we care.” Foreign gain without citizen gain is a stop sign.

World-leading AI — truth-seeking, under humans

Key decisions are stress-tested by world-leading AI on national and provincial extreme supercomputers — built with the best global partners under Canadian control. The analysis is unrestricted by fashion: no political filter that hides risk, no ban on inconvenient questions, no “uncensored” as chaos — uncensored as truth-seeking, deep, historical, and steelman-complete. A Human Council of Logic & Integrity monitors the machines. Named ministers remain accountable. AI proposes and detects. Humans dispose and answer. No social-credit state.

What the law becomes

Here is the country under the Maple Leaf — stated plainly. Beside it, the ground we inherit, so the change is clear. We do not measure ourselves against anyone else. We simply are.

The ground we inherit

  • Food labels exist; full processing byproducts and chemical honesty are incomplete; junk ads still flood screens and shelves.
  • Commercial porn is legal and industrial; gambling ads remain a public business.
  • Grade 10 is classroom time; no universal year of service or military discipline.
  • Federal books are opaque in practice; sole-source and unreadable contracts are common.
  • Major decisions often skip a public steelman; fashion and feelings can outrank cause and effect.
  • Asylum and temporary streams are widely used; removals of violent non-citizens are slow; levels not hard-tied to capacity.
  • Income tax still applies to parents of three; no dual-parent zero at three kids for married/common-law.
  • Healthcare is strained; no adult lifestyle merit gate after 25 for non-emergency care.

Under the Maple Leaf

  • Full public food disclosure + total ban on ads for ultra-processed and unhealthy food.
  • Commercial/public porn banned; gambling ads banned.
  • One year mandatory national service instead of grade 10 — training first, not combat by default.
  • Universal Decision Logic on every material act — steelman, history, replicable data, citizens first; truth-seeking AI under a human council.
  • Public Treasury, Trace IDs, open contracts; falsification → investigation and charges.
  • Secure borders; skilled gap-only immigration; asylum not a mass loophole; violent non-citizens removed immediately.
  • Three dependent kids + married/common-law together = zero income tax for both parents.
  • Citizens/PRs only; under 25 full cover; 25+ merit for non-emergency; ER always first.

2. Healthcare reform

Citizens and permanent residents only. Under 25: full public coverage. Over 25: a random merit exam — fit and clean habits get full coverage; real improvement gets partial; preventable bad habits without progress means you pay for non-emergency care. Emergency always stabilizes first.

Federal

Transfer conditions, Canada Health Act reform, tax/HSA tools, national merit standards, open outcome data.

Provincial

Runs hospitals and clinics — merit exams, stepped care (lifestyle before pills), queues fixed in practice.

Both levels

Federal tax room + provincial delivery of merit public care — where reform becomes life-changing.

More — exam design, stepped care
  • Random / short-notice exams + biomarkers so “one clean day” does not game the system.
  • Medical exceptions for real disease and disability — merit is controllable habits.
  • Stepped care: sleep, food, movement, therapy before psychoactive drugs; extreme limits on youth ADHD/depression meds except emergencies.

6. Immigration & secure borders

Immigration serves Canadians first. The border is real. We admit what we do not already have at home in surplus — skill that net benefits citizens — and we refuse what would hollow wages, housing, or the law.

Secure borders

  • Control of entry is not optional. End catch-and-release theatre at the line.
  • End of asylum as a mass loophole. True, verified cases only; hard timelines; failed claims mean removal — not years of residence by delay.
  • Immediate deportation of violent criminal non-citizens. Violent crime triggers removal priority; appeals do not become a multi-year stay by default.

Who may enter

  • Skilled immigration only as the default — high skill that fills a verified gap Canada does not already have in surplus at home.
  • Admission must benefit the country: wages, housing, fiscal balance, and social trust — not lobby fashion.
  • Capacity formula: inflows track housing, hospitals, schools, and labour need. Volume is not a virtue.
  • Temporary worker and student streams capped and audited — not a back door to permanent mass settlement.

Scrutiny, culture, and renunciation

  • Heightened scrutiny for security and loyalty to Canadian criminal law and equality of persons under the law — with independent oversight so screening is not a black box of political favour.
  • Extra scrutiny where home systems normalize violence, unequal legal status of the sexes, honour/izzat coercion, caste discrimination, or deception-as-norm against Canadian criminal and equality law.
  • That includes theocratic doctrines (including strains of Islamic law that place divine rule above the Criminal Code, license violence, or mandate unequal status of women and non-believers) and caste / honour systems that punish exit, “dishonour,” or lower caste. Frame is doctrine and practice, not blood or ethnicity.
  • Entrants must renounce and not practice such rules as a condition of entry and status. Breach is grounds for refusal or removal.

Religion and Canadian law

Freedom of religion does not license violence, unequal treatment under law, or defiance of the Criminal Code. Where a belief system’s binding public rules require that conflict, those rules are not protected practice under Maple policy. The same standard applies to every religion and belief system — no sacred exemption for caste, honour violence, or theocratic supremacy.

7. National service instead of grade 10

Schools by merit and competence. Sex-real fitness standards where safety requires it. Gifted testing.

One year of mandatory national service replaces grade 10 as the great passage of youth — a headliner, not a footnote. Every eligible young Canadian serves: military discipline, fitness, teamwork, civic duty. A year is enough to solidify habits of order without devouring a life. Default is training and contribution, not combat deployment.

Why this serves nearly everyone

  • The young: structure, fitness, skills, networks — a rite of passage that is earned, not bought.
  • Families: less drift into idleness and crime; clearer path after school.
  • Employers: workers who show up, take instruction, and finish tasks.
  • The nation: cohesion, emergency readiness, a reservoir of trained citizens; less need to import “discipline” we refused to teach.
  • Defence: a broader base of trained people without peacetime conscription into combat.
  • Merit education: no identity quotas in grading or gifted access.
  • Fitness: male/female standards where physiology matters for safety.
  • Gifted: monitored ability testing → accelerated tracks.
  • Homeschool: equal tools and exam pathways — end hostility to competent home education.
  • Tracks: civil defence, infrastructure, health support, conservation, or military training path by fit and need.

Federal

National Service Year statute and funding; transfer incentives for merit education; national standards and readiness.

Provincial

Curriculum, schools, delivery of fitness and gifted programs — provinces run the classroom.

Both levels

Federal service that binds the nation + provincial schools that teach and test competence.

More — phase-in, exemptions
  • Phased by age cohort; infrastructure and trainers built before full mandate.
  • Medical and defined hardship exemptions; alternative civilian tracks preferred over pure exemption.
  • NSY is nation-building — not a political re-education camp.

How the deficit shrinks (illustrative annual channels, steady state after phase-in)

  • Open State waste & fraud: ~1.5–3% of federal program expense recoverable → ~$6–15B/yr (mid ~$10B). Probability of ≥$6B with real ledger + contracts: high if enforced.
  • Preventable health load: 15–25% cut in avoidable lifestyle disease pressure on a ~$399B system over a decade → order of ~$15–40B/yr system-wide (fed + prov) mid case ~$25B; federal fiscal share via transfers and tax room is a fraction — model as ~$5–12B federal room + large provincial room.
  • Immigration fiscal & housing pressure: capacity-capped skilled-only + removals reduce housing stress and social program load; illustrative federal net ~$2–8B/yr after phase-in (wide band; depends on levels cut vs status quo).
  • Crime & justice: remove violent non-citizens; prioritize repeat offenders; less alcohol/gambling glamour → mid case ~$1–4B/yr justice + victim-cost relief (system-wide), with large non-fiscal value (safety, insurance, business hours).
  • UPF ad ban + food tax shift: lower future disease (lags 5–15 years); near-term small ad-tax loss, larger long-run health gain. Mid long-run ~$2–6B/yr health system relief.
