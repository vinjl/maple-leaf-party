Three children, zero income tax

2026-07-23 · Dispatch

Three dependent children, while the parents are married or common-law together: zero personal income tax for both parents.

For one or two children: deep relief on the path. This is the arithmetic of a people that intends to continue — not a seasonal rebate, and not a slogan without numbers. It rises with open books and a health system that no longer spends without sense.
