# Ministry of Health

## Mandate

Deliver **citizens- and PR-first** health: under 25 full public coverage; 25+ merit for non-emergency; emergency always first. Reduce preventable load so tax room and queues improve for everyone who keeps the covenant.

## Citizens-first test

Does this make Canadians healthier and freer from preventable system collapse — without socializing unlimited bad habits onto people who take care of themselves?
