# The Maple Leaf — Home Defence & Self-Defence Tools

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Framework draft  
**Depends on:** `00-first-principles.md`, `15-free-speech.md` (separate)

---

## 1. Thesis

Law-abiding **citizens and permanent residents** may possess and carry basic self-defence tools.  
The **home is a castle**. Violent criminals lose eligibility. Misuse is crushed.

---

## 2. Eligibility

| Status | Default |
|--------|---------|
| Canadian citizen | Eligible |
| Permanent resident | Eligible |
| Temporary foreign nationals | No general carry |
| Violent crime conviction (schedule) | Ineligible (term or lifetime by severity) |
| Peace bond / certain orders | Temporary ineligible |
| Under 18 | No adult carry scheme |

---

## 3. Tiers

| Tier | Tools | Public carry | Home |
|------|-------|--------------|------|
| 1 Non-lethal | **Pepper spray/OC**, alarms | **Yes** if eligible | Yes |
| 2 Bladed | Scheduled knives (length/type); ban disguised/offensive designs | **Yes** under simple national rule | Yes |
| 3 Firearms | Lawful **dwelling defence** | Not general street carry in v1 | **Yes** + storage that still allows defence |
| 4 Prohibited offensive | Switchblade-as-weapon class, disguised, etc. | No | Generally no |

---

## 4. Castle doctrine

- No duty to retreat in your dwelling when lawfully present against unlawful entry / imminent unlawful assault  
- Criminal Code self-defence **clarified**  
- Proportional force (not “anyone on the lawn”)  
- Storage rules rewritten so “safe” ≠ “useless in invasion”  

---

## 5. Misuse

Threaten, rob, or assault with any tool → full criminal law + eligibility revoked.

---

## 6. Federal lever

Criminal Code weapons + self-defence (strong federal competence).

---

## 7. One-line

> **Citizens and PRs who aren’t violent felons may carry pepper spray and lawful blades; your home is your castle; criminals still lose.**
