# 08 — Analysis and human oversight

**Version:** 1.1  
**Status:** Design module (party policy depth)  
**Standard:** logic and published analysis under named humans — not rule by machine.

---

## 1. Purpose

Material decisions need hard scrutiny: logic, history, strongest case for and against from every political spectrum, checkable evidence.  
Where tools help stress-test claims, **prompts and sources are published**. A **named official signs**, can reject the output, and answers for the result.

**No social-credit speech scoring. No black-box rule by algorithm.**

Carve-outs (military operations, secret intelligence) stay as in open-state doctrine.

---

## 2. What is required

| Rule | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Human pen** | Tools may analyse; humans dispose |
| **Public chain** | Analysis used for a Decision Package is published (method, sources, limits) |
| **Uncensored argument** | No fashion filter that hides risk or foreign interest when livelihoods are at stake |
| **Kill criteria** | Failed programs and failed logic die on the record |

---

## 3. Oversight

Named ministers remain accountable. Independent review challenges reckless automation of decisions that belong to elected or statutory officers.  
Detail of any compute infrastructure is **implementation**, not the public promise. The public promise is **logic + open books + human sign-off**.
