## 1. Child tax power

| Children | Federal income tax relief |
|----------|---------------------------|
| 1–2 | **Deep stacked deduction** per child (path to zero) |
| **3 dependent children** | **Zero personal income tax for both parents** if **married or common-law together** (federal; Maple provinces match) |
| 4+ | Additional modest support without infinite loopholes |

Stacks with IDS bonus room per child and Maple Seed contributions.

**Logic:** Three kids while building a household together is nation-building. Both parents zero-rated on income tax — not one.

## 5. National Youth Fitness Standard (NYFS)

| Element | Design |
|---------|--------|
| Sex-based | Separate **male** and **female** standards by age band |
| Battery | Endurance, strength, mobility; open published protocol |
| Exceptions | Disability, serious medical, temporary injury — adaptive goals |
| Sex category | Biological sex for standards/sport; clinical DSD protocol |
| Fail | Remediation path, not pure shame |
| Data | Aggregates public; individual private |

**Healthcare deal:** free catastrophic care for citizens is a shared pool — raising fit kids (when able) is part of holding up the nation’s end. Never deny child emergency care. Support first.

## 6. National Service Year (NSY)

| Element | Design |
|---------|--------|
| Concept | Replace/fuse grade-10 year with **training + education** — **no combat deployment** |
| Content | Fitness, first aid, emergency response, civic knowledge, discipline, basic fieldcraft |
| Credits | Diploma credits (civics, PE, leadership, practical STEM) |
| Alternative | Civilian national service (disaster, conservation, hospitals) |
| Phase | (1) Voluntary pilot with incentives → (2) provincial compact → (3) mandate only if proven **[VERIFY Charter]** |

**Do not launch** with “force all teens into boot camp” as headline. Lead with competence and adulthood.
