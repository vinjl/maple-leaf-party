# The Maple Leaf — Free Speech Act Doctrine

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Framework draft  
**Depends on:** `00-first-principles.md`

---

## 1. Thesis

> **If it is not an explicit call for or threat of physical violence, the criminal law stays out of your mouth.**

Subjective “hate,” hurt feelings, and administrative truth ministries are incompatible with a logic party. Offence is not a crime.

---

## 2. Only criminal speech restriction (ideas)

| Crime | Not a crime |
|-------|-------------|
| Explicit advocacy or threat of **physical violence** against a person or identifiable group | Insult, blasphemy, offensive politics, comedy, demographic argument, historical debate |
| Speech as instrument of conspiracy to commit violence | “Dignity” and vibe-based hatred standards |

**Standard:** objective, narrow, explicit — not adjudicator feelings.

---

## 3. What is abolished / gutted

| Target | Action |
|--------|--------|
| Expansive criminal hate-propaganda (viewpoint police) | Repeal or rewrite to **violence-only** |
| Human rights **speech** complaints for opinions | End jurisdiction over pure expression (keep conduct: housing/employment discrimination) |
| Online-harms regimes forcing platforms to censor legal speech | Replace with violence, CSAM, fraud only |
| State “disinformation” speech licensing | Kill — government argues in public |

---

## 4. Not “subjective hate” — different categories that remain

| Category | Why |
|----------|-----|
| CSAM | Crime against children |
| Fraud, perjury | Conduct / legal process |
| Civil defamation | Optional civil remedy for false **facts**; anti-SLAPP reform |
| Commercial ad rules (junk food, gambling, drugs) | Commercial market regulation ≠ political opinion ban |
| Commercial porn public dissemination ban | Commercial/public sexual market rules — definitions tight so no backdoor speech code |

---

## 5. State behaviour

- No jawboning banks/hosts to kill legal speech  
- Schools teach debate; no mini tribunals for politics  
- Public servants: workplace rules ≠ national speech code  

---

## 6. Legislative skeleton

1. **Maple Free Speech Act**  
2. Criminal Code amendments (violence-only)  
3. Consequential HR code and online-harms repeals  

---

## 7. One-line

> **Total freedom of speech for citizens — only explicit calls for physical violence remain criminal.**
