## 3. CAD Resource Settlement Doctrine (flagship)

### 3.1 Principle

**Canada’s major natural resources are national leverage.** Selling them exclusively (or overwhelmingly) for foreign currency when we could deepen CAD demand is strategic malpractice.

**Maple rule:**

> **Major Canadian resource commodities and strategic materials are to be contracted, invoiced, and settled in Canadian dollars** — with narrow, time-limited exceptions only.

### 3.2 Scope (“major resources”)

Publish a **Strategic Commodity Schedule**, e.g.:

- Crude oil and major refined products  
- Natural gas / LNG  
- Uranium and nuclear fuel cycle products under Canadian control  
- Critical minerals (lithium, nickel, copper, rare earths, potash, etc. as listed)  
- Large-scale electricity export contracts  
- Major bulk grains/oilseeds/fertilizer export contracts above thresholds  
- Other commodities added by statute/order with parliamentary visibility  

### 3.3 Who must comply

| Actor | Obligation |
|-------|------------|
| Federal export licensing / trade tools | Condition market access tools on CAD settlement where constitutional |
| Federally regulated aspects of trade/finance | CAD clearing rails, reporting |
| Crown participants | Full CAD settlement |
| Provincial resource owners / private producers | **Compact + incentive stack** (see §3.5) — Ottawa cannot magically rewrite all private contracts alone, but can make CAD settlement the **profitable default** |
| Buyers (foreign) | Obtain CAD via FX markets / Canadian banks → **structural CAD demand** |

### 3.4 How buyers get CAD (this is the point)

Foreign importers must:

1. Sell goods/services or capital for CAD, **or**  
2. Buy CAD on FX markets, **or**  
3. Hold CAD balances in Canadian banks, **or**  
4. Invest in Canadian assets  

→ **Permanent bid under the Canadian dollar** tied to real molecules and tonnes.

### 3.5 Implementation path (federalism-real)

Provinces own most resources. Maple does not bluff.

**Federal tools:**

- Export permit regimes where they exist or can be designed for strategic goods  
- Tax/royalty **preferences** for CAD-settled contracts (federal tax surface)  
- Trade finance and EDC-class tools **preferencing CAD invoices**  
- Public procurement of shipping/corridor access preferences  
- CRPF and federal partner projects CAD-only  
- Diplomatic standard terms in state-to-state energy/mineral MOUs  

**Provincial compact:**

- Matching royalty discounts or regulatory fast-lanes for CAD-settled books  
- Shared branding: “Canadian Resources — CAD Settlement Standard”  
- Open dashboard of % export value settled in CAD (Open State)

**Phase-in:**

| Phase | Target |
|-------|--------|
| 1 | All new federal/Crown resource-linked contracts CAD-only |
| 2 | Critical minerals + uranium + major energy: CAD default in new contracts |
| 3 | ≥X% of scheduled commodity export value CAD-settled (ratchet up) |
| 4 | Exceptions rare, public, time-limited |

### 3.6 Exceptions (narrow)

- Legacy contracts until roll-off  
- Genuine emergency diversion  
- Small-ticket below de minimis  
- Specific ally mutual-settlement pilots **if reciprocal CAD/other gains** — not permanent USD laziness  

**Anti-pattern:** silent re-invoicing in USD with CAD sticker fiction. Ledger and customs/tax reporting must match.

### 3.7 Interaction with US trade

Maximize US trade and volumes. CAD settlement is **not anti-US**; US buyers are sophisticated FX users. If anything, it professionalizes Canada as a **currency power**, not a branch-plant invoice clerk. Reciprocal free trade remains the goods/services goal; **invoice currency is Canadian strategy**.

## 4. Minimal inflation doctrine (hardcore)

### 4.1 Bank of Canada

- **Primary and overriding mandate: price stability / minimal inflation**  
- Target regime: hard low band (policy draft direction: **≤2%**, with ambition toward **true price stability culture**, not “2% is a floor to inflate away debt”)  
- Operational independence on instruments; Parliament sets the hard goal in statute  
- **No** QE-as-lifestyle; **no** monetizing federal deficits  
- Public reaction function; AI State can publish real-time inflation nowcasts for citizens (transparency, not BoC capture)

### 4.2 Fiscal steel (non-optional partner)

Inflation is often fiscal. Maple:

- Operating surplus path after transition  
- Debt/GDP declining  
- Emergency deficits time-boxed on Open State scoreboard  
- Healthcare redesign + waste purge so the central bank is not asked to clean up Parliament’s mess  

### 4.3 Money and credit

- Reject permanent emergency credit culture  
- Banking regulation that prevents boom-bust taxpayer put options without killing capital magnet  
- Clear separation: **strong CAD ≠ artificially jacked overnight rate forever** — strength from real demand + low dilution  

### 4.4 Metrics

| Metric | Target spirit |
|--------|----------------|
| CPI / core vs statutory band | Inside; miss = public accountability |
| Inflation expectations (markets/surveys) | Anchored low |
| Seigniorage abuse indicators | Near zero |
| CAD real effective exchange rate resilience | Up with productivity |
