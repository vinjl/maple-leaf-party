## 1. Stepped Care Canada — natural first, drugs last

| Step | Default |
|------|---------|
| 1 Foundations | Sleep, whole food, daylight/sun, movement, social connection, off alcohol/nicotine |
| 2 Behavioural / structured natural | Exercise Rx, CBT, light therapy, coaching, school/environment fix |
| 3 Non-drug clinical | Physio, dietitian, monitored programs |
| 4 Pharmaceuticals | After failure/severity/emergency |

**Not** crystal woo instead of sepsis care. **Yes** stop pills-first for mild mood and attention issues.

### Youth psychoactive Rx — extreme restrict

Especially depression and ADHD meds for minors:

- **Not first-line** under 18  
- Mandatory documented lifestyle + therapy + environment trial first (except true emergencies)  
- Under 13: near-prohibition; national exceptional pathway only  
- ADHD: educational/behavioural supports first; specialist + second opinion + reassess  
- Ban DTC/school-adjacent marketing of these drugs  
- Open aggregate Rx rates by region  

Emergencies remain: psychosis, dangerous mania, life-threatening depression, epilepsy, etc. — time-limited specialist care.

## 2. Food transparency

Mandatory digital + package path for covered foods:

- Full ingredients  
- Processing aids/byproducts above thresholds  
- Additive IDs + purpose  
- Nutrition + major origin where feasible  
- **Food Ledger** (public, machine-readable)  
- QR → full composition

## 5. Kids + UPF

- Under 13: ban excessive UPF in schools/daycare/funded youth programs; restrict kids-product junk sales  
- Parental **nutrition duty of care**: support first; sanctions only for proven neglect after help — **not** BMI-only “abuse” crime  
- Medical exceptions documented

## 6. Harm-tiered substances

| Age | Substances |
|-----|------------|
| **25** | Alcohol, nicotine, cannabis; **LSD, MDMA, ketamine** (extreme purity, unit dose, limits) |
| **21** | Psilocybin mushrooms; **coca leaf only** (not cocaine) |
| Illegal | Street cocaine, fentanyl, mystery analogues as party supply |

Purity, purchase limits, no youth ads, tracked legal supply. Medical tracks separate.

### Pregnancy — zero tolerance

| Offence | Rule |
|---------|------|
| Alcohol while pregnant | **Illegal** (knowing use) |
| Nicotine while pregnant | **Illegal** (knowing use) |
| Any other non-prescribed drug while pregnant | **Illegal** |
| Knowingly smoking / vaping nicotine **in presence of a pregnant woman** | **Crime** |

Prescribed medical treatment under physician authority remains lawful. Cessation and treatment support offered — not a free pass to continue harm.
