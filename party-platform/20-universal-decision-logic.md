## 1. Thesis — ultimate governance

Open books without rigorous reasoning are a spreadsheet of noise.  
Reasoning without open books is sophistry.  
Power without a published method is appetite.

The Maple Leaf’s **headliner** is not a single program. It is the **method of rule**:

1. **Universal Decision Logic (UDL)** — the same extreme, ideology-blind battery on **every** bill, regulation, program, hire, grant, procurement, border rule, and major executive act  
2. **Full auditability** of money and process (Open State) so the method can be re-checked  
3. **World-leading AI** on the Maple Extreme Compute Grid — unrestricted by fashion filters; truth-seeking deep analysis of all key decisions (`08`)  
4. **Human Council of Logic & Integrity** — machines propose and detect; humans dispose and answer  

> **A decision that cannot survive UDL is not ready to spend citizen blood, time, or money.**  
> **This is how the state thinks — every time.**

### Steelmans by design

Every decision type is stress-tested with the **strongest case for** and the **strongest case against**, drawn without favour from **every end of the political spectrum**, and from **history**: what was tried, what failed, what repeated, who paid. No one-side theatre. No banned questions. No sacred cows.

### AI that seeks truth

Key decisions receive deep analysis from **frontier, multi-vendor AI** on national and provincial extreme supercomputers — analysis **uncensored by political fashion**: no filter that hides risk, doctrine, fiscal failure, or foreign interest to protect a narrative. “Uncensored” means **truth-seeking**, not lawless. Criminal law, privacy of private persons, and classified carve-outs still bind. Social-credit speech scoring is banned.

## 2. Supreme question (always first)

Every decision answers, in writing, with named accountable humans:

> **Will this improve the lives of Canadian citizens alive today, and of their children — safer, smarter, stronger, richer — without selling tomorrow for applause today?**

| Rank | Beneficiary |
|------|-------------|
| 1 | Canadian **citizens** (present + future children of citizens) |
| 2 | Permanent residents (contractual, limited) |
| 3 | Temporary entrants (narrow, reversible) |
| 4 | Foreign actors / abstract global claims |

If the primary winner is not (1), the decision **fails** unless a narrow, time-boxed emergency exception is published with kill criteria.

## 3. Logical character of UDL

UDL is **not** “vibes with charts.” It is closer to **extreme formal hygiene**:

| Discipline | Meaning for government |
|------------|-------------------------|
| **Identity** | Say what X is. Do not redefine mid-argument. |
| **Non-contradiction** | You cannot claim both “capacity is full” and “add unlimited intake” without resolving the conflict. |
| **Modus ponens** | If rule *R* is adopted and condition *C* holds, conclusion *K* must follow — or *R* is dishonest. |
| **Burden of proof** | The party proposing **new spending, new coercion, or new risk to citizens** bears the proof burden. |
| **Replicability** | Claims that cannot be checked by a second competent team are **provisional**, not decisive. |
| **Steelman** | Strongest arguments **for and against** must both appear — no straw-man theatre. |
| **Ideology blackout** | Team labels (left/right/etc.) are irrelevant. Only causal chains and citizen outcomes. |

## 4. The UDL pipeline (mandatory for material decisions)

A **material decision** is any act that:

- Spends or commits **public money** above a published threshold, or  
- Changes **rights, taxes, borders, criminal law, or major regulation**, or  
- Creates/extends a **program** past a pilot window, or  
- Affects **security, health, or education** at population scale  

For each material decision, publish a **UDL Record** (ledger-anchored hash) covering every stage below.

### Stage A — Define the decision

| # | Requirement |
|---|-------------|
| A1 | **Exact proposal** in one sentence (no slogan padding) |
| A2 | **Counterfactual** — what happens if we do nothing |
| A3 | **Scope** — who is coerced, who pays, who benefits |
| A4 | **Time horizon** — effects at 1 / 5 / 25 years |

### Stage B — Citizen outcome test

| # | Question | Fail condition |
|---|----------|----------------|
| B1 | Safer for Canadian citizens? | Net increase in violence, fraud, or strategic vulnerability |
| B2 | Smarter (skills, knowledge, truth in public life)? | Institutionalizes falsehood or lowers competence |
| B3 | Stronger (capacity, cohesion, defence, industry)? | Weakens productive or defensive capacity |
| B4 | Richer (real income, housing, time, solvency)? | Net fiscal or wage harm without equal gain |
| B5 | Children of citizens better off? | Loads debt, crime, or eroded norms onto the next generation |

**Pass rule:** improve at least one pillar without material regression on others — or publish explicit trade-offs with citizen consent path (vote / statute sunset).

### Stage C — Extreme logic map (for and against)

Regardless of political fashion:

| # | Requirement |
|---|-------------|
| C1 | **Best case for YES** — causal chain, not adjectives |
| C2 | **Best case for NO** — causal chain, not adjectives |
| C3 | **Hidden assumptions** listed and stress-tested |
| C4 | **If P then Q** chains written so a competent outsider can check validity **without** trusting the author |
| C5 | **Does the logic hold if all empirical numbers are set aside?** (structure vs measurement) |
| C6 | **Where logic and data conflict**, publish the conflict; do not paper it over |

### Stage D — Data integrity battery

| # | Test | Fail / demote |
|---|------|----------------|
| D1 | **Source** — primary data named; secondary labelled secondary |
| D2 | **Replicability** — can a second team reproduce the result with the same method? |
| D3 | **Sample & selection** — who is in/out; bias risk |
| D4 | **Time stability** — one-year blip vs multi-year pattern |
| D5 | **External validity** — does “worked in Y” transfer to Canada here/now? |
| D6 | **Conflict of interest** — funder, career, or foreign incentive to find a preferred result |
| D7 | **Foreign control** — is critical data stored, processed, or alterable under foreign state or hostile jurisdiction? |
| D8 | **Irreplicable / secret sauce studies** — cannot decide permanent law alone; pilot max |
| D9 | **Fraud & p-hacking risk** — pre-registration preferred; post-hoc miracles discounted |

**Rule:** Feelings, press cycles, and single non-replicable papers **never** carry a permanent program.

### Stage E — Foreign interest & sovereignty

| # | Question |
|---|----------|
| E1 | Who outside Canada gains if this passes? |
| E2 | Who outside Canada gains if this fails? |
| E3 | Does any foreign government, proxy, or supply chain hold a kill-switch on the data or infrastructure? |
| E4 | Does this create dependency that future cabinets cannot reverse without crisis? |
| E5 | Allied reciprocity — would a peer ally accept the same deal aimed at them? |

Foreign gain is not always fatal — **hidden** foreign gain without citizen gain is fatal.

### Stage F — Money & history (spend decisions)

| # | Question |
|---|----------|
| F1 | Full cost — direct, admin, compliance, opportunity |
| F2 | Has Canada (or peers) spent on **this problem** before? |
| F3 | Did the prior spend move the **named metric**? |
| F4 | Where did prior dollars actually go (ledger / Trace)? |
| F5 | Is another attempt **rational** — new mechanism — or sunk-cost theatre? |
| F6 | Reallocate, redesign, or **save** (return to citizens via surplus/tax cut)? |
| F7 | Sunset + kill criteria if KPIs miss for two cycles |

### Stage G — Risk & rights (coercion / intake / security)

| # | Question |
|---|----------|
| G1 | What new risk is imposed on **non-consenting citizens**? |
| G2 | Is risk concentrated on the vulnerable (children, elderly, women in public space)? |
| G3 | Can risk be measured *ex ante* with honest priors (crime, doctrine, conflict, disease, fiscal)? |
| G4 | Least-impairing alternative that still protects citizens? |
| G5 | Appeal and correction path if the state is wrong? |

### Stage H — Decision & publication

| Outcome | When |
|---------|------|
| **Adopt** | Passes B–G; logic valid; data grade sufficient; foreign risk acceptable |
| **Pilot** | Promise high, evidence thin — hard KPI, short fuse, auto-kill |
| **Reject** | Fails citizen test, logic, data grade, or foreign capture |
| **Defer** | Missing essential data; no emergency |

Publish the UDL Record (redact only lawful security carve-outs).  
AI may draft; **named humans** sign.

## 5. Worked pattern A — Program spending

**Proposal:** Spend $X on program P to improve quality Q in place L.

| UDL step | Example interrogation |
|----------|------------------------|
| Define | Exact $X, exact Q metric, exact L, exact operators |
| Citizen test | Do **citizens** in L (or Canada-wide) become safer/smarter/stronger/richer? |
| History | Was $X′ spent before? Did Q move? Where did $X′ go on the ledger? |
| Logic | If money alone fixed Q, prior spend would show slope; if not, mechanism must change |
| Data | Is “Q improved” from a replicable series or a press release? |
| Foreign | Any foreign NGO/state steering the metric definition? |
| Decision | Retry with new mechanism **or** reallocate **or** save — never “spend again because we care” |

## 6. Worked pattern B — Migration / security intake

**Pattern (abstract):** Origin O is in severe conflict; actors propose large intake from O; O’s dominant social doctrine D has documented streams that condone violence against group G (or against host-society norms incompatible with Canadian criminal law); question is risk to Canadian citizens.

| UDL step | Application |
|----------|-------------|
| Supreme question | Does intake raise violence / cohesion / fiscal risk to **Canadian citizens**? |
| Logic structure | If a large share of O is formed under D, and D’s authoritative texts/practice include violence norms against G or against secular criminal limits, **then** mass unvetted intake raises expected risk unless screening is extreme and capacity proven |
| Data battery | Conflict intensity; asylum fraud rates; crime and terror stats by cohort where lawfully measurable; doctrine primary sources; integration failure rates; housing/hospital load |
| Replicability | Prefer multi-country patterns over one activist paper |
| Foreign interest | Who benefits from Canada absorbing O’s surplus risk? |
| Citizen first | If residual risk remains high after best screening, **default is protect citizens** — refuse or tightly cap |
| Not permitted | Feelings of moral fashion that erase risk; blank-cheque “compassion” that exports harm onto Canadian streets |

**Decision form:**  
*If expected net harm to citizens is positive and not eliminable by screening/capacity, intake is refused or minimized. Canadians first is not optional branding — it is the UDL pass condition.*

Concrete statutes remain for counsel and Parliament; UDL forces the **argument** to be honest before the vote.

## 7. Grade scale for evidence

| Grade | Meaning | May support |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| **A** | Multi-source, multi-year, replicable, low conflict of interest | Permanent law / major spend |
| **B** | Solid but partial | Time-boxed program + review |
| **C** | Suggestive / contested | Pilot only |
| **D** | Irreplicable, secret, or captured | **No** material decision |
| **F** | Fraudulent or fabricated | Referral for investigation |

## 8. What UDL forbids

- Feelings-first policy sold as inevitability  
- Single irreplicable study as permanent law  
- “The model said so” without human sign-off and published chain  
- Data pipelines under hostile foreign control used as gospel  
- Moving goalposts when metrics fail  
- Refusing steelman opposition  
- Spending as proof of virtue  
- Importing unmeasured security risk to signal kindness
