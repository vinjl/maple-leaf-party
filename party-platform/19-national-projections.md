## 2. Deficit-reduction channels (steady state, annual)

All mid cases assume enforcement is real, not paper.

| Channel | Low | Mid | High | Notes |
|---------|-----|-----|------|-------|
| Open State waste/fraud recovery | $6B | $10B | $15B | ~1–3% of federal program spend; needs Trace + contracts |
| Health system pressure relief (all gov) | $15B | $25B | $40B | 15–25% of preventable load on ~$399B; multi-year lag |
| Of which federal fiscal room | $5B | $8B | $12B | Transfers + tax room; province gets large share |
| Immigration levels + skill filter | $2B | $5B | $8B | Housing/social pressure; wide band |
| Crime / justice / victim costs | $1B | $2.5B | $4B | Removals + repeat offenders + alcohol second-order |
| UPF ad ban + food shift (long run) | $2B | $4B | $6B | Health lag 5–15 yrs |

**Mid stack (federal-leaning sum of mid channels, careful not to double-count health):**  
Rough federal-oriented mid: **$10B waste + $8B health room + $5B immigration + $2B crime ≈ $25B/yr** after full phase-in.  
**Low ~$14B · High ~$39B** (overlapping uncertainty).

### Path: deficit → immense surplus → SWF (mid narrative)

| Horizon | Story vs baseline drift |
|---------|-------------------------|
| Y1 | Noticeable laws (food ads, porn/gambling ads, border enforcement, ledger); waste hunt starts |
| Y1–3 | −$10–25B (mid −$15B) deficit pressure from waste + borders + early enforcement |
| Y4–8 | Health/food/crime/productivity compound; service cohorts accumulate |
| Y10 aim | **Immense fiscal surplus** → invest via Canadian **sovereign wealth fund (SWF)**; not annual fashion spend |
| Scoreboard | **GDP per capita** (not just GDP); **CAD among strongest**; **minimal inflation**; **crime slashed** vs drift |

**Tax reform timing:** 3-kid zero and deep rate cuts after Batch 2 cost tables close — funded by load reduction + growth, not pure borrow.

**Service:** mandatory **one year instead of grade 10** — after 5–10 years of cohorts, national cohesion and order support the prosperity path.

**Porn ban:** reduces aggression and lost productivity; benefits compound as youth grow up unexposed — requires **patience to heal**.

## 3. Dollar strength (qualitative + directional)

| Force | Effect on CAD / real purchasing power |
|-------|----------------------------------------|
| Smaller structural deficits | Less monetization pressure |
| Low-inflation mandate | Holds real wages and savings |
| Resources settled in CAD | Structural demand for CAD |
| Capital stays (open fair contracts, competitive rates) | Inflows |

**Probability language:** If fiscal path sticks, **P(real CAD stronger vs loose-deficit baseline over 10 years) > 0.5** — teaching judgment, not a hedge fund call.

## 4. Crime & social second-order effects

| Policy | Mechanism | Expected direction | Confidence |
|--------|-----------|--------------------|------------|
| Remove violent non-citizens | Fewer of that offender set on streets | ↓ victimization | High (identity of set is definitional) |
| Repeat-offender priority | Higher expected punishment | ↓ reoffending rates | Medium |
| Secure borders / skilled-only | Less capacity shock; less gang import risk | ↓ crime intensity, ↑ wage floor | Medium |
| Alcohol/cannabis age 25 + gambling ad ban | Less youth initiation; less alcohol-linked assault | ↓ alcohol-related crime over generation | Medium–low near term; higher long lag |
| UPF ad ban | Less childhood junk demand | ↓ obesity trajectory → health | Medium long lag |
| National service (1 year) | Fitness, order, cohesion, employability | ↓ disorder; ↑ readiness | Medium civic; hard $ |

## 6. Food advertising (policy lock)

**Total ban** on advertising ultra-processed and otherwise unhealthy food products (all major channels). Sale may remain; **the industrial pitch dies**. Aligns with site Food reform pillar.

---

## 7. National service (policy lock)

**One year mandatory** national service = **headliner**. Military discipline and habits without peacetime combat default. Cohesion, stability, employability — in everyone’s long interest.
