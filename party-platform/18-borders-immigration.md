## 1. Metaphor (public)

> Leaves may fall from a tree if exposed to the elements. A people that will not guard its canopy will not keep its shade.

---

## 2. Secure borders

| Rule | Design |
|------|--------|
| Control of entry | Real border control; end catch-and-release as norm |
| Asylum | True, verified cases only; hard timelines; failed claim → removal |
| Violent criminal non-citizens | **Immediate removal priority**; appeals do not default to multi-year stay |
| Temporary streams | Worker/student capped and audited — not permanent mass back door |

---

## 3. Who may enter

| Rule | Design |
|------|--------|
| Default | **Skilled only** |
| Gap test | Admit what Canada does **not** already have in surplus at home |
| Benefit test | Net benefit to citizens: wages, housing, fiscal, trust |
| Capacity formula | Volume tracks housing, hospitals, schools, labour need |

---

## 4. Scrutiny & renunciation

| Rule | Design |
|------|--------|
| Heightened scrutiny | Security + loyalty to Canadian criminal law & equality of persons |
| Independent oversight | Publish standards; audit favouritism; cleared review of secrets |
| Practice filters | Violence; sex-unequal legal status; honour/izzat coercion; caste; deception-as-norm where conflict with Canadian law |
| Theocratic / Islamic law strains | Where doctrine places divine rule above Criminal Code, licenses violence, or mandates unequal status of women/non-believers — **heightened scrutiny**; renounce & non-practice as condition |
| Caste / honour systems | Same: renounce and not practice |
| Frame | **Doctrine and practice**, not ethnicity or blood |

---

## 5. Religion and Canadian law

Freedom of religion **does not shield**:

- Violence  
- Unequal legal treatment of persons (e.g. by sex)  
- Defiance of the Criminal Code / equality norms as binding public rule of a creed  

Where a creed’s **binding public rules** require that conflict, those rules are **not protected practice** under Maple policy. Same standard for **every** religion and ideology.

**Charter / international law:** careful drafting required. Intent = rule-of-law compatibility and citizen security — **not ethnic purity**. **[VERIFY / counsel]**

## 6. Federal / provincial

| Level | Role |
|-------|------|
| Federal | Borders, levels, selection, asylum, removals, citizenship, inadmissibility |
| Provincial | Honest capacity signals (housing, health, schools, labour gaps); local interfaces |
| Both | Federal line control + provincial truth = immigration that fits the country |
