**Version:** 0.1

## 1. Why this document exists

Logic parties die when they promise federal magic in provincial fields. Maple tags every major plank:

## 4. Sequencing power

Some reforms are **unilateral federal** day-one capable (tax, open ledger for federal spend, immigration levels, BoC mandate statute, federal election law).

Others are **compact-dependent** (healthcare delivery reality, resource projects on provincial land).

Maple governing strategy:

1. Day-one unilateral wins that prove seriousness (Open State federal, immigration levels, fiscal map).  
2. Parallel provincial compact offers with money attached.  
3. CHA/tax landmark bills with public scoreboard.  
4. Do not pretend a federal party can rewire hospitals by press release.
