# The Maple Leaf — Corporate Tax: Canada-First + HQ Magnet

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Framework draft  
**Depends on:** `03-capital-magnet-tax.md`, `07-currency-resources.md`  
**Goal:** Compete with **Ireland** and **UAE** for real HQ/IP — reward profits that **stay and pay Canadians**.

---

## 1. Thesis

> **If your profits build Canada and pay Canadians, tax is light. If you strip Canada, tax is not. Come for real substance — we’ll still win on rate.**

---

## 2. Dual-track system

| Track | Who | Tax direction |
|-------|-----|---------------|
| **MDRT** — Maple Domestic Reinvestment Track | Canadian-substance firms that retain/reinvest in Canada and/or pay Canadian residents | **Minimal / near-zero** federal CT on retained & reinvested earnings; very low on qualifying Canadian operating profit |
| **Outbound / strip track** | Earnings upstreamed to non-resident parents, low-substance shells | Higher effective rate; anti-hybrid |
| **FHRT** — Foreign HQ Relocation Track | Multinationals that **genuinely** move HQ/IP/jobs to Canada | Time-boxed **landing rate** competitive with Ireland/UAE + Canadian premium (law, banks, USMCA, resources) |

---

## 3. MDRT qualification tests (bright lines)

1. **Substance** — real decision-makers / payroll / offices in Canada  
2. **Reinvestment** — capex, R&D, Canadian wages, Canadian M&A above threshold of earnings  
3. **Distribution skew** — dividends/buybacks mostly to **Canadian residents** *or* retained (not primarily stripped out)  
4. **Optional IP nexus** — IP income tied to Canadian R&D (substance over sticker)

Fail → standard track. Filings machine-readable; aggregates on Open State.

---

## 4. Dividends

| Flow | Treatment |
|------|-----------|
| To Canadian residents from MDRT cos | Enhanced integration / low personal tax |
| Outbound to non-residents | Withholding; MDRT denial on stripped earnings |
| CCPCs | Simplified MDRT — not Big-4-only |

## 5. Beating Ireland & UAE

| Their edge | Maple answer |
|------------|--------------|
| Ireland low CT / IP-HQ | Competitive landing CT + real nexus + CUSMA access + English common law |
| UAE low tax / capital park | CSRCP + banking fortress + hard CAD + Open State trust + FHRT substance HQ |
| Speed | Binding rulings in 30–60 days |

**Not** pure letterbox. **Yes** Switzerland/Ireland hybrid with resources and US adjacency.
