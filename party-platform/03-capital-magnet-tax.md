## 1. Problem

Canada loses people, founders, and capital to the United States because **after-tax keep-rates, capital-market depth, and build-speed** often win south of the border. Guilt does not reverse capital flight. **Outcompetition** does.

Simultaneously, high personal taxes are locked in by **high permanent spending**, especially an open-ended healthcare basket and bureaucratic sprawl. Maple’s tax cuts are funded by **spend redesign** (see Health Covenant + waste purge), not fairy dust.

---

## 2. Design goals

1. **Workers keep more** of what they earn.  
2. **Savers and investors** are rewarded — money invested is not treated like money blown.  
3. **Parents** get large, simple tax relief per child.  
4. **Global clean wealth** chooses Canadian banks and Canadian deployment.  
5. **Citizens remain first** — capital residency ≠ mass immigration or housing predation.  
6. **Legal and clean** — CRS/FATCA-aware; AML serious; no laundromat brand.  
7. **Predictable** — no surprise confiscation (kills Swiss credibility overnight).

---

## 3. Philosophy in one line

> **Lightly tax production, saving, Canadian reinvestment, and child-rearing. Fund a thin, serious state from residual consumption and unavoidable rents — after the free minor-care healthcare circus is gone.**

---

## 4. Sequence (non-negotiable for credibility)

| Phase | Action |
|-------|--------|
| 1 | Open Ledger baseline of all federal spend |

## 5. Personal tax for citizens & residents — “Work, Invest, Family”

### 5.1 Work — major effective tax cut on labour

**Direction (parameters to cost in shadow budget):**

- Dramatically expand the **basic personal amount / zero bracket** so a full-time median worker keeps a far larger share of earnings.  
- Flatten and lower remaining brackets once healthcare savings land.  
- Target philosophy: competitive with high-growth US jurisdictions **after** Maple’s redefined benefit stack (catastrophic health + open state + safety).

**Metric:** median full-time take-home pay ↑; net migration of high earners to US ↓.

### 5.2 Invest — Investment Deduction System (IDS)

**Core rule:** Qualifying amounts **saved/invested** in approved Canadian-substance vehicles are **deductible from taxable income** in the contribution year (subject to rails).

#### Qualifying contributions (illustrative menu)

- Broad Canadian listed equities / Canadian-equity ETFs  
- Canadian private business equity (holding-period rules)  
- Canadian bonds and productive infrastructure vehicles  
- Designated build-to-rent / housing-**supply** vehicles (not flip speculation shelters)  
- Approved productivity / resource development funds  

#### Account mechanics (hybrid to finalize in costing)

Two viable sub-models (pick primary after revenue model):

| Model | Contribution | Growth | Withdrawal |
|-------|--------------|--------|------------|
| **A — Super-RRSP** | Deductible | Sheltered | Taxed as income (progressivity preserved later) |
| **B — Super-TFSA+** | Deductible up to cap | Sheltered | Tax-free if rules met (more pro-saver, costlier) |

**Maple preference for v1 modelling:** Model A for high caps (protects long-term revenue), with a Model B **tier-1** smaller tax-free sleeve for middle earners — best of both worlds without unlimited free lunch.

#### Behavioural goal

- Invest / save → taxable income falls now.  
- Blow income on pure consumption → residual tax still applies.  
- Creates cultural and arithmetic preference for **capital formation in Canada**.

#### Guardrails (mandatory)

1. **Annual rails:** % of earned income and/or hard dollar caps (citizens get higher caps than temporary residents).  
2. **Lifetime / carryforward rules** so people are not trapped by one bad year.  
3. **Canadian economic substance** for aggressive private vehicles.  
4. **Anti-round-trip:** no interest-deduct + contribute games without tight limits.  
5. **Offshore strip clawback:** if capital is extracted contrary to lockup rules, prior deductions recaptured with interest.  
6. **Machine-readable reporting** to CRA; aggregate stats public (Open State) without doxxing individuals.  
7. **No unlimited deduction** that zeroes tax for the ultra-rich without CSRCP/levy interaction rules.

### 5.3 Family — children multiply relief

- Large **per-dependent-child deduction** (or refundable/non-refundable credit — prefer simple deduction stacked with IDS for clarity).  
- **Bonus IDS room** per child (family capital formation).  
- Review household taxation so stable two-parent households are not punished relative to split structures.  
- **Metric:** child-related effective tax rate ↓; fertility-capable household disposable income ↑.

### 5.4 Residual tax base funds only the thin state

After Work/Invest/Family:

Defence, borders, justice, catastrophic health, debt service, core infrastructure, Open State operations.

Boutique credit maze → deleted wherever IDS + family deduction supersede them.

## 6. Canadian Strategic Resident Capital Program (CSRCP)

### 6.1 Purpose

Make Canada the preferred **legal custody and growth base** for mobile high-net-worth individuals (HNWI / UHNWI), thickening Canadian banks and markets — **without** selling passports or detonating urban housing.

### 6.2 Who qualifies

- High net worth above a published threshold (tiered)  
- **Source-of-funds** verified to serious AML standards  
- Security and character screens (espionage, organized crime, sanctions → hard no)  
- Not a substitute for skilled immigration quotas for ordinary labour

### 6.3 What they receive

| Benefit | Design |
|---------|--------|
| Legal certainty | Multi-year contracts of tax treatment; no surprise raids |
| Banking custody | Full access to Canadian regulated institutions as safe harbour under Canadian law |
| Tax simplicity on foreign portfolio wealth | See §6.5 primary model |
| Competitive treatment | Canadian-source investment returns designed to attract **deployment into Canada** |
| Privacy | Strong protection against private snooping / frivolous exposure; not protection from serious crime process |

### 6.4 What they do **not** receive

- Automatic or toy citizenship  
- Federal voting rights without a separate, real integration path  
- Unlimited right to warehouse empty urban housing  
- Any implication that Canada hides assets from legitimate serious criminal courts

### 6.5 Tax model options (pick one primary after modelling)

| Option | Idea | Pros | Cons |
|--------|------|------|------|
| **1. Annual flat Resident Capital Levy** | Pay a published annual levy scaled to declared global wealth under Canadian custody; foreign portfolio income otherwise lightly touched | Simple, predictable, easy to brand | Calibration hard; wealth valuation fights |
| **2. Remittance / non-dom analogue** | Tax foreign income only if remitted; Canadian income taxed normally; annual charge for status | Known international pattern | Complexity; abuse if remittance defined poorly |
| **3. Territorial features** | Foreign active business lightly taxed if substance abroad; Canadian activity taxed here | Attracts HQs carefully | Base-erosion risk |

**Maple v1 recommendation:** **Option 1 (levy) as primary** + mandatory minimum **Canadian productive deployment** each year (see §6.6). Simple Swiss-adjacent pitch: *pay a clear price for the fortress; invest here; sleep at night.*

### 6.6 What Canada requires in return

1. **Minimum custody:** capital parked in Canadian regulated institutions.  
2. **Minimum deployment:** % of capital into Canadian productive assets (business equity, resources, infrastructure, approved housing **supply** vehicles) over rolling windows.  
3. **Substance:** real presence days **or** active Canadian investment office/management presence — not pure brass plate.  
4. **Annual compliance fee** funding enforcement and AML.  
5. **Housing firewall** (below).  
6. **Exit rules:** modest, predictable — not confiscatory — so entry is rational.

### 6.7 Housing firewall (citizen-first)

Global wealth must not win by buying the starter-home stock out from under citizens.

- Primary residence allowance for CSRCP participants who actually live here  
- Tight limits / surtaxes on non-resident and empty speculative urban housing  
- Channel incentives to **build-to-rent, industrial, resource, and plant** investment  
- Municipal/provincial cooperation via federal tools where available; public scoreboard of outcomes

### 6.8 Banking brand

Canadian banks + OSFI-class regulation marketed as:

> **Safest serious North American legal custody for global capital** — paired with open government of the *state*, not a public strip-search of every honest account.

AML for crime/terror remains aggressive. That is a feature of the Swiss-grade brand, not a bug.

## 8. Interaction with the United States

- Maximize trade and investment with **reciprocity**  
- Do not build a system that only works as tax fraud against the IRS — it will be crushed and will trash Canada’s reputation  
- Win **location decisions**: HQ, talent, family office, custody, plant  
- If the US turns protectionist, Canada’s answer is **competence + alternative corridors**, not performative anti-Americanism

## 9. Provincial coordination

Personal and corporate tax interact with provincial rates. Maple federal reform:

- Leaves room for **provincial tax competition** (Swiss cantonal analogue)  
- Offers provinces a **coordination compact**: if they lower destructive rates and speed housing/permits, federal infrastructure preference follows  
- Does not require identical provincial policy to launch federal IDS/CSRCP
