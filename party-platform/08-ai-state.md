# The Maple Leaf — AI State Doctrine

**Version:** 0.5  
**Status:** Framework draft — **flagship efficiency pillar**  
**Depends on:** `00-first-principles.md`, `01-governing-doctrine.md`, `05-open-state-architecture.md`, `20-universal-decision-logic.md`  
**Goal:** Make Canada the **world leader in AI-integrated government** — every function, every process, every bureaucracy measured and improved — on **national and provincial extreme supercomputers**, under a **human council**, so the state becomes leaner, fitter, stronger, and able to **cut taxes** from real waste destruction (not fairy dust).

---

## 1. Thesis

Open books without intelligence are a library. Intelligence without open books is a black box.

Maple runs both at **total scope**:

1. **Maple Ledger + Process Registry** — every public dollar and **every government process**  
2. **AI State** — frontier AI that **operates, audits, simulates, and redesigns** *all* registered government functions  
3. **Universal Decision Logic (UDL)** — the same extreme logic battery for every material decision (`20`)  
4. **Maple Extreme Compute Grid** — world-class national + provincial supercomputing so integrity work is not theatre  

**Not:** a chatbot on three websites.  
**Yes:** AI as the default integrity engine for **literally every bureaucratic workflow**, watching for funny business, waste, and failure — with humans still on the hook.

**Citizen interest test:** raise quality and cut waste for Canadians — not social credit, not unaccountable automation, not “the model said so” as a shield for ministers.

## 2. Universal mandate

### 2.1 Rule

> **If a process is in the Maple Process Registry, AI State must be able to observe it, measure it, and (where lawful) accelerate or quality-check it.**

No orphan **civilian** directorates. No “our work is special so we stay paper-only” after transition.

**Carve-out aligned with Open State:** public AI observability does **not** require public disclosure of **military operations** or **secret police / intelligence operations**. Those use accredited **classified AI tiers**, command authority, and cleared oversight — not the public copilot layer.

Everything else: **100%** AI-observable on the public MPR/ledger stack.

## 4. Operating model per process

For each `process_id`:

1. **Observe** — ingest stage events from Maple Ledger  
2. **Predict** — time-to-complete, breach risk, cost overrun  
3. **Assist** — drafts, completeness checks, routing  
4. **Detect** — anomalies vs rubric and historical peers  
5. **Explain** — plain language to citizen/applicant where external  
6. **Propose redesign** — quarterly kill/merge/automate recommendations  
7. **Never own** — named human remains legally accountable  

### Automation tiers (explicit)

| Tier | Meaning | Allowed when |
|------|---------|----------------|
| **0 Monitor** | Metrics only | Always |
| **1 Assist** | Drafts for human | Default |
| **2 Bound auto** | Auto-approve within hard rules | High-volume low-risk; appeal intact |
| **3 Restricted** | Classified / weapons / core rights | Special accreditation + dual control |

Tier 2+ requires published rules, eval harness scores, and kill switch.

## 5. Hard rules (AI constitution for the state)

1. **Human accountability** — named official per decision class  
2. **Explainability for adverse actions** — reasons + appeal  
3. **No secret binding law** — rules that affect rights are published (or logged security exception)  
4. **Open evaluation** for civilian systems  
5. **Ledger-native** — money and process events always emitted  
6. **No social credit** — ban general political/speech citizen scoring  
7. **Privacy minimization**  
8. **Kill switch & version rollback**  
9. **Multi-vendor / no monopoly lock-in**  
10. **Canadian control for critical paths**  
11. **Universal registry coupling** — no AI on unregistered ghost processes  
12. **Bureaucracy reduction bias** — AI success = fewer layers, not more dashboards

## 6B. Maple Extreme Compute Grid (national + provincial)

### Mission

Build and operate **world-class extreme supercomputing** for government integrity and decision quality — not prestige vanity clusters that sit idle.

| Layer | Role |
|-------|------|
| **National flagship** | Federal AI State Platform: whole-of-government audit, UDL stress-tests, fraud/waste graphs, simulation |
| **Provincial nodes** | Same standards for provincial ledgers/processes; federation of integrity, not thirteen incompatible toys |
| **Classified tier** | Air-gapped capacity for defence/intel carve-outs only — still human-commanded |

**Primary jobs of the Grid:**

1. **Monitor the entire non-exempt government** — money, process, contracts, KPIs  
2. **Catch funny business** — anomalies, sole-source patterns, shell NGOs, duplicate grants, ghost employees  
3. **Eliminate waste** — continuous kill-list of failed programs and redundant layers  
4. **Raise efficiency** — SLA collapse, fewer stamps, faster citizen services  
5. **Run Universal Decision Logic at scale** — every material decision stress-tested (`20`)  
6. **Feed tax reduction** — publish recoverable waste → surplus path → lower rates (see fiscal doctrine)

### Partnerships (tools, not masters)

Canada invites **best-in-world builders** under hard contracts:

| Partner class | Examples (illustrative) | Terms |
|---------------|-------------------------|--------|
| Compute / silicon | e.g. NVIDIA and peers | Canadian-soil deployment, export-control clean, open interfaces |
| Frontier labs | e.g. xAI, Anthropic, OpenAI and peers | Competitive multi-vendor; eval harness; no single-vendor prison |
| Canadian industry & universities | Domestic labs, energy, cold-climate data centres | Preference where equal or better on security + performance |

**Sovereignty rules (non-negotiable):**

- Critical weights, logs, and citizen data under **Canadian legal control**  
- Foreign staff and remote admin **cleared and logged**; no silent foreign kill-switch  
- Multi-vendor; escrow / exit plans  
- Public model cards for civilian systems; classified systems under cleared review only  
- UDL + ledger always above any vendor brand  

Partnership is how a middle power gets **frontier capability fast**. It is not how Canada outsources judgment.

### Energy & siting

Hydro, nuclear, and clean firm power corridors; cold-climate efficiency; provincial competition to host nodes under federal security standards.

### Success = leaner state, not bigger dashboard bureaucracy

If admin cost, process layers, and failed programs do not fall, the Grid has failed — regardless of FLOPS press releases.

| **Ministry AI leads** | Publish inventory: every process → AI tier |

**Ban:** shadow AI on sensitive data outside ASP accreditation.

### Human Council of Logic & Integrity

AI does not rule Canada. **Citizens’ servants do.**

| Rule | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Monitor** | Council reviews AI State actions, Grid use, and high-impact UDL recommendations |
| **Challenge** | Must steelman opposition; can send decisions back for rework |
| **Veto automation** | Can freeze Tier 2+ automation and any AI-directed spend hold |
| **No rubber stamp** | Minutes public (non-exempt); dissent recorded |
| **Composition** | Fixed terms; competence in logic, security, fiscal, law, engineering — not donor fashion |
| **Cannot hide** | Ministers remain legally accountable; Council is oversight, not a scapegoat |

> **AI proposes and detects. Humans dispose and answer.**
