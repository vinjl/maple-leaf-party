## 1. Thesis

Open books without intelligence are a library. Intelligence without open books is a black box.

Maple runs both at **total scope**:

1. **Maple Ledger + Process Registry** — every public dollar and **every government process**  
2. **AI State** — frontier AI that **operates, audits, simulates, and redesigns** *all* registered government functions  

**Not:** a chatbot on three websites.  
**Yes:** AI as the default co-pilot and integrity engine for **literally every bureaucratic workflow**.

**Citizen interest test:** raise quality and cut waste for Canadians — not social credit, not unaccountable automation, not “the model said so” as a shield for ministers.

## 2. Universal mandate

### 2.1 Rule

> **If a process is in the Maple Process Registry, AI State must be able to observe it, measure it, and (where lawful) accelerate or quality-check it.**

No orphan **civilian** directorates. No “our work is special so we stay paper-only” after transition.

**Carve-out aligned with Open State:** public AI observability does **not** require public disclosure of **military operations** or **secret police / intelligence operations**. Those use accredited **classified AI tiers**, command authority, and cleared oversight — not the public copilot layer.

Everything else: **100%** AI-observable on the public MPR/ledger stack.

## 4. Operating model per process

For each `process_id`:

1. **Observe** — ingest stage events from Maple Ledger  
2. **Predict** — time-to-complete, breach risk, cost overrun  
3. **Assist** — drafts, completeness checks, routing  
4. **Detect** — anomalies vs rubric and historical peers  
5. **Explain** — plain language to citizen/applicant where external  
6. **Propose redesign** — quarterly kill/merge/automate recommendations  
7. **Never own** — named human remains legally accountable  

### Automation tiers (explicit)

| Tier | Meaning | Allowed when |
|------|---------|----------------|
| **0 Monitor** | Metrics only | Always |
| **1 Assist** | Drafts for human | Default |
| **2 Bound auto** | Auto-approve within hard rules | High-volume low-risk; appeal intact |
| **3 Restricted** | Classified / weapons / core rights | Special accreditation + dual control |

Tier 2+ requires published rules, eval harness scores, and kill switch.

## 5. Hard rules (AI constitution for the state)

1. **Human accountability** — named official per decision class  
2. **Explainability for adverse actions** — reasons + appeal  
3. **No secret binding law** — rules that affect rights are published (or logged security exception)  
4. **Open evaluation** for civilian systems  
5. **Ledger-native** — money and process events always emitted  
6. **No social credit** — ban general political/speech citizen scoring  
7. **Privacy minimization**  
8. **Kill switch & version rollback**  
9. **Multi-vendor / no monopoly lock-in**  
10. **Canadian control for critical paths**  
11. **Universal registry coupling** — no AI on unregistered ghost processes  
12. **Bureaucracy reduction bias** — AI success = fewer layers, not more dashboards

- Tool APIs into case systems, payments, HR, permitting

- Immigration points for verified elite technical talent (still capacity-capped overall)  
- Sovereign compute on Canadian soil  
- Fast energy (nuclear/hydro/gas) for compute corridors — resource power feeds AI power  

### Procurement

- Buy measured KPI deltas on real processes, not demos  
- Open interfaces mandatory  
- Kill failed IT megaprojects using AI risk scoring
