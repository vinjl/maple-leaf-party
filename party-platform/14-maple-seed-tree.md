## 1. Thesis

Replace Canada’s education-only **RESP** with a two-stage **lifetime capital vehicle**:

```
Birth → MAPLE SEED (parent/guardian) → age 18 → MAPLE TREE (adult child)
```

| Promise | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **In** | Contributions **deductible** (within high rails) |
| **Inside** | Growth **100% tax-free** |
| **Out** | Withdrawals **100% tax-free** — never forced onto the child’s income like RESP EAPs |
| **Purpose** | **Any legal use** — not school-locked |
| **Invest** | Fully custom brokerage **or** one-click **CRPF** (sovereign wealth) sleeve |
| **At 18** | Auto-convert to child-controlled **Tree**: liability shield, borrow-against, zero purpose limits |

**Public line:** *Plant a Seed. Grow a Tree. Every Canadian child a capital owner.*

## 2. Why RESP dies

| RESP flaw | Maple fix |
|-----------|-----------|

## 3. Maple Seed (ages 0–18)

### 3.1 Opening

- Citizen child (SIN) — primary  
- Opens at birth or on registration  
- Controller: parent/guardian as **fiduciary** for the child  

### 3.2 Contributions

| Who | Rule |
|-----|------|
| Parents, grandparents, guardians | Yes |
| Child (earned income) | Yes |
| State match | Optional modest match for low-income citizen families |
| Deduction | **100% deductible** to **contributor’s** taxable income in year paid (within rails) |

### 3.3 Rails (honest fiscal design)

**Not** “infinite billionaire dump with no bound.”  
**Yes** best-in-world **high** annual + lifetime ceilings, indexed, higher than historic RESP/TFSA; anti-abuse for non-substance dumps.

Publish: *Effectively unlimited for normal and ambitious Canadian families; ceilings exist so the treasury remains a country, not a trough.*

### 3.4 Investments

- Custom: listed securities, ETFs, bonds under ordinary rules  
- **CRPF / Maple Nation sleeve:** units in Canadian Resource Prosperity Fund  
- Optional age-based default glide path  
- Ban: pure casino products, self-dealing junk, unregistered pump schemes  

### 3.5 Withdrawals (Seed years)

- Guardian may withdraw for child’s benefit under fiduciary duty  
- **All tax-free** to beneficiary — **no income inclusion**  
- At majority: remaining balance → Tree (no taxable deemed disposition)

## 4. Maple Tree (18+)

### 4.1 Control

- Adult child **100% controller**  
- Parent control ends (except voluntary gifts back into Tree under rules)

### 4.2 Tax charter (continues)

- Growth tax-free  
- Withdrawals tax-free, any legal purpose  
- Further contributions from adult’s income: deductible within rails (map clearly vs IDS/TFSA/RRSP)

### 4.3 Liability shield (“citizen capital”)

Statutory protection similar to enhanced trust/RRSP-style exemption:

**Generally not available** to ordinary unsecured creditors / opportunistic personal suits.

**Exceptions (public list):** criminal proceeds/forfeiture, fraud, family support court orders, tax on non-Tree income, deliberate evasion schemes.

**Do not promise:** immunity from all courts forever.

### 4.4 Borrow against

- Regulated Canadian lenders may offer **Tree-secured / securities-backed loans**  
- Published interest and margin rules  
- Default remedies limited to collateral under statute  

### 4.5 Estate / family law

- Named successor; probate-light transfer  
- Federal tax shield ≠ erase provincial family law — coordinate division rules
