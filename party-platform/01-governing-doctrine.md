## 1. Government as an open source project (measurable system)

Ministries are not moral fashion houses. They are **operators** with budgets, outputs, and kill criteria — run with **open source project discipline**:

| Maxim | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Transparent** | Public by default (non-exempt) |
| **Open source** | Taxpayer software, schemas, process specs inspectable |
| **Secure** | Real engineering + disclosure; obscurity only for military ops / secret intel |
| **Lean** | Delete dead programs/processes; small surface area |
| **Integrity max** | Immutable history, named maintainers, dual control, reproducible totals |

Detail: `09-gov-as-open-source.md` · ledger/process: `05-open-state-architecture.md` · AI CI layer: `08-ai-state.md`

Clear mission, **every non-exempt dollar on the Maple Ledger**, **every non-exempt process in the MPR**, **AI State efficiency**, competitive procurement, no permanent programs without permanent proof.

## 2. Spending doctrine — Necessary vs noise

### 2.1 Maximize (core state)

- Sovereignty, defence, Arctic/NORAD/cyber competence  
- Borders and immigration enforcement  
- Justice system speed and seriousness (courts, prosecutions, corrections for violent crime)  
- Critical infrastructure and export corridors  
- Catastrophic **citizen** health insurance (see Health Covenant)  
- Disaster and pandemic surge capacity  
- Strategic science, dual-use tech, and **sovereign AI/compute** where markets under-provide  
- Permitting systems that decide **yes/no fast** (AI-assisted)  
- **Open State infrastructure** (Maple Ledger) as core state capacity  
- **Hard CAD / resource settlement rails** as strategic economic capacity

### 2.2 Minimize / eliminate (noise)

- Duplicate agencies and overlapping grant empires  
- Perpetual pilots with no outcome metrics  
- Corporate welfare without equity warrants / clawbacks  
- Ideological and activist pass-through funding without citizen ROI  
- Vanity internationalism disconnected from Canadian interest  
- Opaque management consulting as a shadow public service  
- Free minor/routine healthcare for all (exits public basket)  
- Any program that fails its own published KPIs for **two consecutive** evaluation cycles

### 2.3 New program rule (non-negotiable)

No new federal program without all of:

1. Problem statement and counterfactual  
2. Full cost (including admin)  
3. Measurable success criteria  
4. Sunset date or automatic review with kill default  
5. Public dashboard from day one  
6. Named accountable official

## 3. Fiscal rules (Swiss-NA credibility)

1. **Publish the map first** — full baseline spend on the Open Ledger.  
2. **Cut architecture** — waste purge + healthcare basket redesign.  
3. **Lock rules** — multi-year operating balance path; falling debt/GDP glidepath published.  
4. **Then cut taxes** — permanent rate reform after spend truth is visible.  
5. **No monetized deficits** — fiscal and monetary credibility are the currency brand.

**Exception handling:** true national emergencies may pause balance rules for a fixed window with automatic expiry and public scoreboard.

## 4. Tax doctrine (summary — detail in capital-magnet paper)

- Prefer **low rates + few loopholes** over high rates + lobbyist maze.  
- Reward **work, Canadian reinvestment, and raising children**.  
- Treat saved/invested income preferentially vs pure consumption (Investment Deduction System).  
- Invite clean global capital under substance rules; never surprise confiscation.  
- CRS/FATCA-aware: legal magnet, not fraud magnet.

## 5. Markets doctrine

| Default | Exception (state acts) |
|---------|-------------------------|
| Free private markets | Natural monopoly regulation |
| Consumer choice | Catastrophe risk pooling |
| Competitive entry | National security / critical supply |
| Price discovery | Clear externalities (e.g. pollution) with least-cost tools |

**Industrial policy test:** If subsidizing, require public bid, measurable output, clawbacks, and preferably equity participation for the public. No blank cheques to “champions.”

## 6. Procurement doctrine

1. **Open tender default** — sole-source is exceptional, time-limited, publicly justified.  
2. **Published rubrics** — price + quality + track record; no vibe awards.  
3. **Full contract text public** — amendments and change orders included.  
4. **Performance scores public** — chronic failure → debarment.  
5. **Best value** = cheapest way to hit a **specified** quality bar, not cheapest fiction that fails delivery.  
6. **Canadian preference only** where capability truly exists for defence/critical systems; otherwise best ally bid.

## 7. Open State doctrine (summary — detail in `05-open-state-architecture.md`)

**Every single government dollar** and **every single government process / bureaucratic function**:

- **Dollar Trace ID** — appropriation → payment → delivery → outcome (zero threshold) for **literally every outflow**, including **all benefits**  
- Benefits/person payments: **anonymized fully public auditable** events (recompute totals; no public identity)  
- **Maple Process Registry (MPR)** — if staff time or a decision exists, it has a `process_id`, owner, SLA, cost model, and public plain-language description  
- **Live stage events** on Maple Ledger for every case (privacy-tiered)  
- Unregistered processes / off-ledger non-exempt payments illegal after transition  
- Contracts, bids, lobby registry, beneficial ownership of payees  
- Government software **and AI systems** open/auditable by default  
- Machine-readable statutes and regs  

**Only full carve-outs from public 100%:** **military operations** and **secret police / security-intelligence operations**. Everything else is fully open. Personal privacy protects case *payloads*, not whole ministries. Misuse of carve-outs is an offence.

**Blockchain role:** integrity infrastructure for **all non-exempt public money and processes** — not token salaries, not public ballots, not crypto monetary cosplay.

## 7b. AI State doctrine (summary — detail in `08-ai-state.md`)

- World-leading AI on **every registered process** — not three pilot chatbots  
- Observe, speed, quality-check, anomaly-detect, propose kill/merge of bureaucracy  
- Human-named accountability for every automated decision class  
- No social-credit political scoring  
- Success = fewer layers and lower cost per case, not more dashboards

## 8. Elections doctrine

- Franchise: **citizens only** for federal elections.  
- End-to-end verifiable (E2E-V) design: cast-as-intended, recorded-as-cast, tallied-as-recorded — **without** revealing how you voted.  
- Open-source election software; independent audits; paper / risk-limiting audits as appropriate.  
- Public bulletin board (ledger-compatible) for cryptographic commitments.

---

## 9. Federalism doctrine

Respect provincial jurisdiction. Prefer:

- **Compacts** and transfer conditions over theatre  
- Federal action where constitutional power is clear (tax, criminal law, immigration, trade, banking, defence)  
- Enablement of resource development (corridors, export markets, investment climate) rather than Ottawa cosplaying as mine operator  

Every major platform plank tags: **Federal action / Provincial ask / Metric**.

---

## 10. Regulatory doctrine

- Time-limited decisions (“yes means yes” clocks)  
- One-window review for major projects  
- Cost-benefit and cumulative burden analysis  
- Sunset for regs that fail their stated purpose  
- No regulation whose only real function is to export industry to worse jurisdictions while claiming virtue

---

## 11. Human capital doctrine

- Public service hiring: competence and integrity first  
- Fireability for chronic non-performance in senior ranks  
- No racial spoils; equal opportunity under law  
- External experts welcome under conflict-of-interest glass box

---

## 12. Crisis protocol

1. Define the threat in falsifiable terms  
2. Time-box emergency powers  
3. Publish costs and collateral damage weekly  
4. Prefer targeted tools over economy-wide shutdowns when evidence allows  
5. Automatic expiry + parliamentary reauthorization  
6. Post-crisis audit on the Open Ledger
