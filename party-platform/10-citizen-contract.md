# The Maple Leaf — Citizen Contract

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** People-facing framework  
**Party:** The Maple Leaf (not yet a registered party — political platform)

---

## What this is

A plain contract between **Canadian citizens** and a Maple Leaf government.  
Not poetry. What you get. What you owe. What the state may never do.

---

## 1. The deal in one paragraph

Canada is run for **citizens first**. Government is an **open source project**: every non-exempt dollar and process is auditable; you can file bugs; fraud is public. You keep more of what you earn if you work, save, invest, and raise children. Healthcare is serious insurance for bad luck if you hold up your end — not a free junk-food-and-pills unlimited buffet. The dollar is hard. Speech is free unless you call for violence. Your home is your castle.

---

## 2. What you get

### 2.1 Open government

- Follow **every non-exempt public dollar** (including benefits — anonymized, fully auditable)  
- Every bureaucratic process registered, timed, owned  
- **Citizen Bug Tracker (MCIP):** report bugs, suggest fixes, expose waste/fraud  
- Enough citizen signatures → escalation, up to lawful referendum  
- Only black boxes: **military operations** and **secret intelligence operations**

### 2.2 Money and family

- Lower tax on work after waste is cut  
- **Invest → taxable income falls** (Investment Deduction System)  
- **Three dependent children + married/common-law together = zero personal income tax for both parents**; deep relief on the path to three  
- **Maple Seed → Maple Tree:** deductible contributions, tax-free growth, tax-free withdrawals, any purpose; at 18 the child owns a Tree (shield + borrow)  
- Optional piece of the **national resource wealth fund (CRPF)**

### 2.3 Health and food

- Public care for **serious** illness/injury for **citizens** who keep the **Health Covenant**  
- Emergency stabilization for citizens always  
- Minor care not free-at-point-of-use forever  
- **Full food disclosure** (ingredients, processing byproducts, chemicals)  
- Unhealthy consumable ads restricted; **gambling ads banned**  
- Healthy/organic food tax-favoured; ultra-processed taxed harder  
- **Lifestyle first, drugs last** — especially for kids’ mood/attention meds  
- Boys and girls: honest **male/female fitness standards** at school (disability exceptions)  
- **Pregnancy:** illegal to use alcohol, nicotine, or non-prescribed drugs while pregnant; crime to knowingly smoke near a pregnant woman

### 2.4 Rights and safety

- **Free speech:** only **explicit calls/threats of physical violence** are criminal speech crimes — not “hate feelings”  
- **Self-defence tools** for citizens and PRs (no violent-crime conviction): pepper spray, scheduled knives; home firearms + **castle doctrine**  
- Borders and immigration: secure borders; capacity-capped; skilled gap-filling only; violent non-citizens removed; law over creed (see `18-borders-immigration.md`)  
- Verifiable elections  

### 2.5 Kids and future

- Subsidized **AI tools** under 18 and for post-secondary students  
- Homeschool respected; **gifted path** via monitored ability tests  
- National Service Year **replaces grade 10** (training, not combat deployment); mostly sex-separated streams; grade 11–12 after  
- Substance ages by harm: **25** alcohol, smoked nicotine, cannabis, LSD/MDMA/ketamine (pure); **21** psilocybin, smokeless tobacco, coca **leaf** (not cocaine)  
- Children’s clothing standards toward organic / natural fibre where exposure matters  
- **Waste bounty:** verified citizens paid when findings cut real tax spend

### 2.6 Builders

- If company profits **stay in Canada**, reinvest, pay **Canadians** → **light corporate tax**  
- Real foreign HQs welcome (compete with Ireland/UAE on substance + rate)  
- Hard CAD; major resources settled in Canadian dollars

## 3. What you owe (citizen side)

1. **Lawful conduct** — crime is not a lifestyle brand  
2. **Health Covenant** basics if you want free catastrophic coverage (with medical exceptions)  
3. **Truth in claims** on benefits and taxes — fraud is public and punished  
4. **Parents:** duty to feed real food and support fitness when able — support first, not BMI cruelty  
5. **Speech:** freedom includes others’ freedom; violence advocacy is out  
6. **No free ride on the commons** — temporary entrants are not equal to citizens for the full stack  

---

## 4. What the state may never do

- Hide non-exempt spending or processes  
- Run subjective speech police for legal opinions  
- Deny emergency care to a citizen child as punishment  
- Confiscatory surprise wealth raids (kills the Swiss brand)  
- Sell citizenship as a toy to capital  
- Pretend unlimited free everything is compatible with a hard currency

- “Best in world” accounts still need **anti-abuse rails** so the country stays solvent  
- Hard policies (service year mandate, commercial porn ban) are phased and Charter-tested
