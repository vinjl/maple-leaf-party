## Preamble

The Maple Leaf governs for **Canadian citizens first**: open-source government, hard CAD, capital that stays Canadian, families with real tax and Seed→Tree power, stepped-care health (lifestyle first, drugs last), free speech except explicit violence, and a state you can file bugs on.
