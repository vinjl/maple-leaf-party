# Charter pathway — youth & harmful substances

Policy and legal design track for the Maple Leaf: restrictions on harmful substances for people under 25, grounded in developing-brain evidence and Charter reasoning (ss. 7 and 15).

## Framing

Child protection and public health first — not moral theatre. See also `party-platform modules on food/health` and platform modules on food, health, and substances (`party-platform/11-food-health-nation.md`).

## Status

Living research track. **Not** a filed court application. Uncertain legal claims must be marked `[VERIFY]` and reviewed by counsel before any public legal use.
